## Why these examples

- They cover three source types: text, video transcript, and audio/podcast transcript.
- They use public original sources, not private local files.
- They show the final reader-facing article mode, not intermediate source maps or audit logs.
- They demonstrate the no-bloat revision principle: add source-supported detail when it improves the article, but do not dump every note into the final prose.
