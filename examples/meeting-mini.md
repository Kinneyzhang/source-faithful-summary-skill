# Mini Example: Meeting Transcript

This fictional example shows how the skill separates decisions, suggestions, and action items.

## Source excerpt

Maya: We need to decide whether to launch the beta on Friday.

Leo: The backend is ready, but analytics events are still incomplete. I can finish them by Thursday afternoon.

Priya: I suggest we invite only existing customers first. If we open it publicly, support will get flooded.

Maya: Let's launch Friday to existing customers only. Leo owns analytics by Thursday 5 p.m. Priya, please draft the customer email by Wednesday. Public launch is not decided; we'll revisit after support metrics from the beta.

## Weak generic summary

The team decided to launch the beta Friday and prepare analytics and customer emails.

## Source-faithful meeting summary

### Decision

- Launch the beta on Friday, but only to existing customers.
- Public launch is not decided and will be revisited after support metrics from the beta.

### Action items

- Leo: finish analytics events by Thursday 5 p.m.
- Priya: draft the customer email by Wednesday.

### Reasoning / constraints

- Backend is ready.
- Analytics is incomplete but can be finished before launch.
- Public launch may flood support, so the beta scope is limited.

### Not a decision

- Public launch was discussed but explicitly deferred.
